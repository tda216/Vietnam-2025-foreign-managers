# Philippines FY2025 research instructions

Apply the shared research and evidence rules in the repository's parent `AGENTS.md`, with this Philippines-specific change directed by the research owner on 5 November 2026:

- Foreign ownership may be measured at **any date from 1 January through 31 December 2025**; it is no longer restricted to year-end. Record the actual measurement date for every observation. Publication in 2026 is acceptable when the ownership measurement date is in 2025. Keep multiple observations rather than silently selecting a date or averaging them; review any classification conflict around 50%.
- FY2025 includes an eligible role held at any time in 2025, including a role that ended during the year. Record the resignation/departure time whenever the source provides it, at the precision the source supports; keep it blank when undisclosed. Do not treat BoardEx's far-future end-date placeholders as actual departure dates.
- The unit of observation is each listed company in the research sample. Attribute a qualifying FY2025 role to the sampled listed company where it was actually held.
- If a subsidiary is itself listed and in the sample, attribute a role held there to **that subsidiary**, not automatically to its listed parent. If the same person holds eligible roles at both sampled listed companies, record the person separately for each company.
- A role held only at an **unlisted subsidiary** is outside the study, regardless of the subsidiary's country. Record an exclusion as `UNLISTED_SUBSIDIARY_ONLY`, with the entity, reason, and source PDF page or workbook row. Preserve the source row. A role at a listed subsidiary outside the study sample is likewise outside this sample.
- If the employing entity or its listing/scope status is unclear, leave eligibility unresolved until verified. Do not exclude a person who also has a separate eligible role at a sampled listed company.
- These Philippines rules supersede the earlier rule that included domestic unlisted subsidiaries. Do not change the Vietnam records or rules for this project.

## Nationality of managers that BoardEx does not disclose (research-owner decisions, 6 November 2026)

- First, look the person up in the company's own FY2025 17-A or Information Statement (`ph11_boardex_nationality_scan.py scan`). Accept automatically only a nationality written directly after the name, in the director/officer formats S1–S5; Filipino results go to `data/manual/boardex_nationality_fill.csv`. Read foreign, dual or conflicting results by hand before recording them.
- Then, for people still undisclosed: **if the name is Filipino, record Philippine nationality** (owner: "nếu tên Philippines thì để quốc tịch Philippines"). Record it as `Nationality_Raw = INFERRED` with `Method = INFERRED_WEAK` (name only). The test is conservative and data-driven: the surname must occur among people stated Filipino and among nobody stated foreign. Surnames shared with foreign nationals (Tan, Lim, Lee, Go, Ng, …) stay undisclosed.
- **Clearly foreign names (owner, 7 November 2026: "nếu tên nước ngoài có thể để found").** After the filing and web search finds no stated nationality, a person with a clearly foreign name may be recorded as foreign (`INFERRED_WEAK`, name only), and the company counts as FOUND. This is allowed **only when a country can be inferred** (e.g. a Japanese, Indian or German name). Otherwise keep the person on `Review_Open`. Ambiguous names stay unresolved, e.g. Chinese-Filipino surnames or married names.
- People on `Review_Open` are to be checked later in the FY2024 / FY2023 17-A or Information Statement (owner, 7 November 2026).
- **Concluding a company (owner, 7 November 2026).** A person whose nationality stays undisclosed keeps a company open only when there is a foreign indication: either a BoardEx foreign national whose role is unverified, or a foreign-looking name on `Review_Open`. If no foreign person is identified, conclude `NONE_FOUND` with `Flag_Basis = NO_FOREIGN_IDENTIFIED_OWNER_RULE`, and do not search further for that group. This matches Vietnam, where only foreign-looking names were candidates.

## Foreign-ownership measure (research-owner decisions, 5–6 November 2026; written to be reusable for other countries)

- **What FO measures: voting power.** FO = share of the votes attached to outstanding shares that is held by holders whose legal nationality is not the study country. Basis: IMF *BPM6*, ch. 6, §6.12 ("Control is determined to exist if the direct investor owns more than 50 percent of the voting power in the direct investment enterprise") and §6.19 ("When decisions are made on a one-share one-vote basis, voting power is in the same proportion as the ownership of ordinary shares"; non-voting shares and shares with higher vote weights make voting power differ from share ownership). Saved copy: `philippines/raw/methodology_refs/IMF_BPM6_chapter6.pdf` (PDF pp. 4–5) with its `.meta.json`.
- **Denominator:** all outstanding share classes that vote in the election of directors: common shares, voting preferred shares, and Class A/Class B voting common. Exclude treasury shares, non-voting preferred, and unexercised warrants, options or convertibles (§6.19). If a class carries more than one vote per share, weight it by votes. With a single voting class (the usual case), FO = foreign % of outstanding common shares.
- **Numerator:** shares whose holder of record has a non-local **legal** nationality, i.e. the citizenship column or place of incorporation written in the filing, not the ultimate owner. Include nominee or custodian accounts that the filing labels foreign (e.g. "PCD Nominee – Non-Filipino"). A broker or custodian block that the filing labels with a nationality takes that label (PPC, 6 Nov 2026).
  - This deliberately differs from BPM6, which follows one investor's control through chains of ownership. Here all foreign holders are aggregated at the first level.
- **Sources, in order (Philippines):** PSE Top 100 (31 Dec 2025) with holder nationality from the FY2025 17-A; issuer statements of foreign ownership (17-A sentence/table, PSE Public Ownership Report with a foreign field, PSE Form 17-13 Foreign Ownership Report); the company's **2025 General Information Sheet** (capital structure Filipino/Foreign, stockholder list with nationality). The GIS may be the main source when the 17-A does not settle the class, and it may identify holders (approved by the owner on 6 Nov 2026; earlier it was a cross-check only). Record the GIS as-of date (annual meeting date or "period covered"), or the filing date if no date is legible.
- **Classes:** FO > 50 → `FOREIGN_MAJORITY`; FO ≤ 50 → `LOCAL`; missing, or bounds that straddle 50 → `REVIEW`. Apply mechanically (BPM6 §6.13: "These definitions should be used in all cases … to avoid subjective judgments").
- **Unknown nationality:** keep bounds. `FO_Lower` treats unknown holders, including an unknown voting-preferred block, as local. `FO_Upper` treats them as foreign. Never set unknown to 0.
- **Recording:** `FO_Method = VOTING_SHARES_BASIS_OWNER_RULE` (or a direct issuer statement on the voting basis) whenever a second voting class exists. In the Note, write the common-only figure too.
- **Still open, for the owner to decide when another country needs it:** non-voting depositary receipts held by foreigners over local-held shares (e.g. Thai NVDRs), golden shares, and multiple-vote shares.

## Career before appointment of foreign executives (research-owner decision, 9 November 2026: option C)

File: `data/manager_background.csv` - all 79 foreign Executive/Other_Manager people at the 35 LOCAL firms, done 9 Nov 2026 (not in the workbook). Sources: 17-A/IS profiles, PSE disclosures, company pages, news; LinkedIn cannot be read without login and is not scraped.
- **Host_Prior_Category** (one value per person, for the 2025 position): `INTERNAL` = held an earlier position at the same listed company; `PH_EXTERNAL` = held a position in the Philippines at another employer before joining; `DIRECT_FROM_ABROAD` = the source describes the career before joining and names only employers outside the Philippines; `NOT_STATED` = the source does not say. An earlier position at a parent, subsidiary or sister company in the Philippines, or in a Philippine business the company acquired, also counts as `INTERNAL` (owner, 9 Nov 2026); name the entity in Prior_PH_Position_Same_Firm. `INTERNAL` takes precedence; the path before joining stays in **Before_Joining** (`PH_EXTERNAL` / `DIRECT_FROM_ABROAD` / `NOT_STATED`), so both readings are kept.
- Reading rules from the 13-person batch (9 Nov 2026): `INTERNAL` needs a stated earlier post (column Earlier_Post_At_Firm = YES); if that is not stated, the category is the Before_Joining value. `DIRECT_FROM_ABROAD` needs the last role named before joining to be outside the Philippines (a role without a location, e.g. "independent consultant", gives `NOT_STATED`). A post at a group company **outside** the Philippines is not a host-country post (tag `GROUP_TRANSFER_FROM_ABROAD`). An internal step known only from BoardEx role dates is accepted, tagged `INTERNAL_FROM_BOARDEX_DATES`. Aggregator sites (MarketScreener, Zonebourse) are not evidence.
- Further rules (9 Nov 2026, full run): an earlier post at the same company that is clearly based abroad (e.g. IMI Europe operations) is treated like a foreign group company: tag `GROUP_TRANSFER_FROM_ABROAD`, category from Before_Joining. If the location of an earlier post at the company is not stated, it still counts as `INTERNAL` (tag `INTERNAL_LOCATION_NOT_STATED`). Other tags: `CITI_PHILIPPINES`, `CITI_ACQUISITION_POSSIBLE` (UBP people who may have moved with the Citi consumer business in 2022 - owner to decide), `LOCATION_FROM_GENERAL_KNOWLEDGE`, `POSSIBLE_NOT_FY2025`, `NATIONALITY_CONFLICT`.
